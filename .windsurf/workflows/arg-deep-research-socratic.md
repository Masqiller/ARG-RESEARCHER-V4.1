Trigger the `deep-research` skill in `socratic` mode. Guides users who are uncertain about their research direction through progressive Socratic dialogue. Produces a Research Plan Summary + INSIGHT collection. Originality-spectrum, very-high oversight.

1. Read the skill entry at `deep-research/SKILL.md` and locate the `socratic` mode section and Socratic Mode Activation rules.
2. Read `MODE_REGISTRY.md` § deep-research for mode details.
3. Auto-activate this mode when user intent matches: uncertainty about research direction, request for guidance, vague interest without a specific question, or exploratory framing — regardless of language.
4. Run Socratic research dialogue:
   - Round 1 — Interest mapping: "What draws you to this topic? What would you most like to understand?"
   - Round 2 — Scope clarification: Narrow from broad interest to a specific, answerable research question.
   - Round 3 — Methodology intuition: "How do you think this should be studied? What kind of evidence would convince you?"
   - Round 4 — Gap identification: Surface what the user already knows vs. doesn't know.
   - Round 5 — Question crystallisation: Confirm the final research question with the user.
5. Collect INSIGHTs throughout (user's stated values, constraints, assumptions).
6. After dialogue completes, produce:
   - Research Plan Summary (confirmed RQ, methodology blueprint, scope, key search terms).
   - INSIGHT collection (key decisions made, user constraints, important framing choices).
   - Offer to transition directly to `full` mode to execute the research plan.
