Trigger the `deep-research` skill in `quick` mode. Produces a concise research brief (500–1,500 words) on any topic. Fidelity-spectrum, medium oversight. Ideal for rapid background summaries or 30-minute overviews.

1. Read the skill entry at `deep-research/SKILL.md` and locate the `quick` mode section.
2. Read `MODE_REGISTRY.md` § deep-research for mode details.
3. Execute a compressed 3-stage pipeline:
   - Stage 1 (Scoping): Brief research question formulation (no extended dialogue).
   - Stage 2 (Investigation): Focused literature search — top 5–10 highest-relevance sources only. Apply source verification (Two-Layer citation protocol v3.7.1).
   - Stage 3 (Composition): Concise brief with: Executive Summary, Key Findings (3–5 points), Evidence Summary, Key Gaps, References.
4. No full synthesis, bias, or ethics pass in quick mode.
5. Brief should be actionable and directly answer the research question.
6. Include AI disclosure statement.
7. Note to user: quick mode trades depth for speed. Use `full` mode for rigorous academic output.
